Degrees of Difficulty

Degrees of Difficulty is my Substack. I take a question about higher education, such as what it is for, who it actually reaches, or why it is built the way it is, and think it through, usually with some history and a little economics. It is written for anyone curious about how the system really works, not only those inside it.
