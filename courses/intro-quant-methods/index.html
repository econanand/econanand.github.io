Introduction to Quantitative Methods

UG · Introductory

An introductory undergraduate course covering the quantitative tools used in economic analysis, with an emphasis on building intuition alongside technique. The notes below were used as in-class material; they include questions intended for class discussion alongside the exposition, and assume the structure of a discussion-led classroom.
