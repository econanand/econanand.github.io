About

Biography

I am an Associate Professor of Economics at the School of Arts and Sciences, Azim Premji University, Bengaluru, and a Faculty Fellow at the Centre for Sustainable Employment. My research sits at the intersection of political economics, development economics, and applied microeconomics — with a particular focus on the causes and consequences of conflict in developing countries, the political economy of natural resources, and the role of identity in politics.

I hold a PhD in Economics from the University of Cambridge (dissertation: "Conflict, Riots and Welfare: Essays on Political Economy and Public Finance in India"), an MPhil in Economic Research and a Graduate Diploma in Economics, also from Cambridge. Before turning to economics I trained as a mechanical engineer at IIT Madras and ran a factory producing instant coffee — a circuitous route that informs my interest in the real-economy dimensions of development.

Research

Research Interests & Projects

Economics of Conflict

Social, political, and economic causes and consequences of civil conflict and communal violence in developing countries.

Political Economy

Electoral politics, natural-resource governance, and the political economy of public finance in India.

Labour Economics

Employment, wages, and the comparability of labour market surveys in India, including public workfare programmes.

Identity, Religion & History

How religion, culture, and colonial legacy shape economic behaviour and outcomes, including mental-health consequences.

Development Economics

Applied microeconomic analysis of poverty, public goods, fiscal transfers, and inflation in the Indian context.

Computational Methods

Quantitative and computational approaches to economic research, including network economics.

Current Projects

  • Religion, Mental Health, and Covid-19 — Examining the relationship between religious affiliation, social networks, and mental health outcomes during the pandemic in India. (with G. Bahal, S. Iyer, and K. Shastry)
  • Labour Market Survey Comparability — Assessing the consistency and comparability of major official labour force surveys in India. (with Rosa Abraham)
  • Fiscal Federalism and Macroeconomic Stability — Studying how intergovernmental fiscal transfers affect inflation dynamics at the state level in India.
